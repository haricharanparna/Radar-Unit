# DELTA PULSE submission video (Remotion project)

- Composition: `DeltaPulseFinal` (1920×1080, 30 fps, 897 frames = 29.9 s), defined in `src/Root.tsx`.
- Timeline: `src/Main.tsx` (one `<Shot>` per shot; timings in seconds on the narration clock).
- Subtitles: `src/captions.json` (generated by `scripts/make_subtitles.py`, identical to `../exports/DELTA_PULSE_FINAL.srt`).
- Footage: `public/recordings/` = copies of `../recordings/` (genuine captures from `scripts/record.mjs`).
- Audio: `public/audio/` (cleaned team narration, original music bed and SFX from `scripts/audio/`).
